Factory Conveyor Network — 3D Assets
Source: https://3dassets.dev/packs/factory-conveyor-network

License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original models are disclosed by their publisher as AI generated.
Downloaded individual GLB models:
26224 Free roller conveyor -> roller-conveyor.glb
26320 Industrial filter bank -> filter-bank.glb
26248 Barcode scanning arch -> sensor-arch.glb

Changes: runtime layout and scale. Sensor arch is a visual analogue in the

All model files are served locally. models.js embeds the same GLB files to support
file:// launch and fallback. Runtime loader: Three.js GLTFLoader r128 (MIT),
covered by vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. No external asset connection is required.
The surrounding process layouts are illustrations, not surveyed Allur factory CAD.
